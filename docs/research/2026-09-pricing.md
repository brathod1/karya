## Patterns in the Indian market

- **Price bands:**
  - Free (ledgers, basic invoicing)
  - **₹1,500–4,000/yr**, the main band for billing apps
  - ₹8,000–10,000/yr for top tiers with several users
- **A permanent free tier is the norm.** Trials, where they exist, are short (7–15 days).
- **Yearly pricing first**, usually with a "struck-through" original price. Yearly billing saves 10–20%.
- **Priced per business, not per user.** Extra users, desktop access and multiple businesses are the paid add-ons.

## Where this product fits

1. **Built around the order, not the invoice.** The Indian tools are built for GST invoicing and stock. None of them makes a custom multi-item order the main record, with advance, part payments, balance due and delivery date. Khatabook tracks who owes money, but has no idea what the order was.
2. **Production planning for events and stalls.** No Indian tool does it. The closest are Stocksmith (materials-heavy, priced in USD) and CakeBoss (bakers only).
3. **WhatsApp / Instagram sellers.** Storefront tools assume catalogue checkout and take a cut of each order. Custom orders arrive in DMs, with per-customer prices. Having no GST or inventory module is a *simplicity advantage* for home sellers.
4. **Small retail is a harder fit.** Walk-in shops usually want quick billing, GST invoices and stock, which is exactly what Vyapar and myBillBook do well and Karya deliberately doesn't. **Home and handmade sellers are the stronger place to start.**

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ₹0 | 1 user, capped active orders (e.g. 20–30/month). Enough to try with real work |
| **Solo** | ₹149–199/mo or ₹1,499–1,999/yr | One owner, unlimited orders, attachments, events |
| **Team** | ₹399–499/mo or ₹3,999–4,999/yr | Several users with roles (staff, viewer / accountant) |

This sits inside the familiar ₹1,500–4,000/yr band, priced per business with extra users as the upsell. **Don't build billing or plan limits yet** (see P4). Test the numbers in conversations first.
