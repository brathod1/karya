# Conversation guide: current client (P0.2)

She is a friend and uses the app for free, so this conversation isn't about charging her. It's about learning three things:
- whether the product is worth paying for
- what's missing
- who else she knows

Keep it casual: chai, 20–30 minutes, and let her do most of the talking. Write notes straight after, into the "Notes" section at the bottom.

**Ground rules**
- Ask about **what she actually did**, not what she'd hypothetically do. "When did you last…" beats "Would you…".
- Don't defend the app or explain features while she's talking. Just note where she got confused.
- Friends are kind. Push gently for the negatives.

---

## 1. How she really uses it

1. Walk me through the last order you took. Where did it come from (DM, call, stall), and what did you do in Karya?
2. Which screens do you open most days? Which have you never opened?
3. Is there anything you still track on paper, WhatsApp or the Google Sheet? Why?
4. Phone or laptop, and which one when you're busy?

## 2. What's painful or missing

5. What's the most annoying thing about using it?
6. Was there a moment you almost gave up, or went back to the sheet?
7. If you could add one thing tomorrow, what would it be?
8. Is anything confusing, like words, numbers or statuses?
9. Do you ever need to send customers something: order details, balance due, a reminder? How do you do it now?

## 3. Is it worth money?

10. If Karya disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead? How bad would that be?
11. What has it saved you (time, missed payments, forgotten orders)? Can you think of a specific example?
12. Do you pay for any other apps for the business (Canva, Instagram ads, billing apps)? How much?
13. Pretend you aren't my friend. What would feel like a fair monthly or yearly price for this? At what price would it feel too expensive?

## 4. Who else?

14. Who do you know who runs a similar business: crochet, crafts, baking, stalls, Instagram sellers?
15. Would you be comfortable introducing me to two or three of them?
16. Which exhibitions, markets or seller groups do they go to or belong to?

## 5. Permission

17. Can I quote you and use your business name (Prishious Knots) on a website as an early user?
18. Can I show screenshots with your data blurred?
